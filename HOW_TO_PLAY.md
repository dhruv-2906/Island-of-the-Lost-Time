# 🎮 How to Play - Island of the Lost Time

**Simple guide to get you started playing the game!**

## ⚡ Quick Start

After opening the game in Unity and pressing Play (▶️):

1. **Move around** with **WASD** keys
2. **Look around** by moving your **mouse** (hold right-click to rotate camera)
3. **Attack enemies** with **Left Click**
4. **Stay near mushrooms** to heal
5. **Defeat enemies** to progress through the story

That's it! You're playing! 🎉

---

## 🎮 Basic Controls

### Movement
- **W** - Move forward
- **A** - Move left
- **S** - Move backward
- **D** - Move right
- **Shift** - Sprint (run faster)
- **Mouse** - Look around
- **Right-click + drag** - Rotate camera
- **Scroll wheel** - Zoom in/out

### Actions
- **Left Click** - Attack (melee)
- **E** - Mount/Dismount horse (when near one)
- **H** - Rally your squad to follow you

### Information
- **Tab** - See your story progress
- **F1** - Get a helpful hint

## 🎯 Your Mission

**The Story:**
You're on a mysterious island that's been corrupted. Giant mushroom mountains and beautiful waterfalls used to be peaceful, but now corrupted guardians roam the land. Your job is to defeat them and restore balance!

**What to Do:**
1. Explore the island
2. Find and defeat corrupted enemies (red creatures)
3. Complete 5 chapters by defeating enemies
4. Restore peace to the island!

---

## ⚔️ Combat Tips

### Fighting Enemies
1. **Get close** to an enemy (within 2-3 units)
2. **Left-click** to attack
3. Each attack deals 25 damage
4. Watch your **health bar** (top-left corner)
5. **Retreat** if your health is low
6. **Heal** near mushroom mountains

### Enemy Behavior
- **Red capsules** = Enemies
- They **patrol** around the map
- They **chase** you if you get too close
- They **attack** when in range
- Some enemies **call for help** from nearby allies

### Winning Fights
- ✅ Keep moving to avoid enemy attacks
- ✅ Use mushroom mountains as healing stations
- ✅ Rally your squad (H) for backup
- ✅ Don't fight too many enemies at once
- ✅ Run away if overwhelmed, then heal

---

## 💚 Healing System

### Mushroom Mountains = Health
- **Giant mushroom mountains** are scattered across the island
- **Stand near them** to heal slowly over time
- Look for the **green healing particles**
- Different colored mushrooms have different properties
- Red, blue, purple, and orange mushrooms all heal you!

### When to Heal
- After combat
- Before fighting tough enemies
- When health bar is yellow or red
- Between story chapters

## 🗺️ Exploring the Island

### What You'll Find

**🍄 Mushroom Mountains**
- Giant mushrooms (15-30 units tall)
- Colorful caps with white spots
- Heal you when nearby
- Safe zones during combat

**💧 Waterfalls**
- Beautiful cascading water
- Peaceful areas
- Good landmarks for navigation
- Usually near elevated terrain

**👾 Enemies**
- Red capsule creatures
- Patrol specific areas
- Become aggressive when you approach
- Drop nothing when defeated (just story progress)

**🐴 Horses (Optional)**
- Mount with **E** key when close
- Move faster when mounted
- Press **Shift** to gallop
- Dismount with **E**

## 📖 Story Progression

### Chapter System
The game has **5 chapters** that unlock as you defeat enemies:

1. **Chapter 1** - Defeat 3 enemies (Mushroom Forest)
2. **Chapter 2** - Defeat 5 more enemies (Waterfalls)
3. **Chapter 3** - Defeat 7 more enemies (Lost Souls)
4. **Chapter 4** - Defeat 10 more enemies (Corruption Source)
5. **Chapter 5** - Defeat final boss (Final Guardian)

### Tracking Progress
- Press **Tab** to see current chapter
- Check how many enemies defeated
- See what's needed for next chapter
- Read chapter descriptions

## 💡 Beginner Tips

### Your First 5 Minutes
1. **Learn the controls** - Walk around, practice camera
2. **Find a mushroom** - Stand near it to see healing effect
3. **Find an enemy** - Practice combat on one enemy
4. **Heal up** - Go back to mushroom after fighting
5. **Explore** - Find more mushrooms and enemies

### Survival Tips
- ⭐ **Always know where the nearest mushroom is**
- ⭐ **Don't rush into enemy groups**
- ⭐ **Use sprint to escape if needed**
- ⭐ **The camera is your friend** - Look around often
- ⭐ **Rally your squad (H)** when facing tough enemies

### Common Mistakes
- ❌ Fighting without knowing where to heal
- ❌ Taking on too many enemies at once
- ❌ Ignoring the health bar
- ❌ Not using sprint to reposition
- ❌ Forgetting about squad rally

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### "I can't move"
- Make sure the game is in Play mode (press ▶️)
- Click on the game view window
- Try pressing WASD again

### "I can't see anything"
- Camera might be in wrong position
- Try right-clicking and dragging mouse
- Use scroll wheel to zoom out

### "Enemies aren't moving"
- NavMesh needs to be baked
- See SETUP_GUIDE.md for instructions
- Or ask for help!

### "I keep dying"
- Stay near mushroom mountains
- Don't fight multiple enemies at once
- Use sprint (Shift) to run away
- Press H to rally your squad

### "Where are the mushrooms?"
- Look for giant colorful structures
- They're 15-30 units tall
- Red, blue, purple, or orange
- Scattered across the map

## 🎮 Advanced Gameplay

### Squad System
- Press **H** to rally AI companions
- They will follow and help you
- Use them for tough fights
- They attack enemies automatically

### Horse Riding
- Approach a horse
- Press **E** to mount
- Use WASD to control
- Press **Shift** to gallop (faster)
- Press **E** to dismount
- You can attack while riding!

### Enemy Types
Different colored enemies have different behaviors:
- **Red** - Mushroom Guardians (guard mushrooms)
- **Blue** - Waterfall Sentinels (near water)
- **Purple** - Lost Souls (aggressive wanderers)
- **Green** - Forest Wardens (patrol areas)
